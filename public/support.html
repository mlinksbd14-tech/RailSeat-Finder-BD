Frequently Asked Questions
How does live seat tracking work?

RailSeat Finder queries official Bangladesh Railway availability feeds in real-time, checking all coach classes and route sectors.

How accurate is the Live Radar map?

Our map geometry is 100% snapped to Google Maps physical railway tracks with sub-meter precision and dynamic compass heading.

How do Seat Drop Alerts work?

Click 'Alert Me' on any train card. When cancelled tickets are released, our background monitor triggers audio alerts and Web Push notifications.
